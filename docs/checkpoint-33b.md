## Checkpoint 33b — comparison pass

This pass changes four existing building assets. All 33 measured assets remain
under comparison; none has been accepted as a perfect replica.

- **Myspace:** separated raised photovoltaic faces from their cast shadows.
  The rejected colour-mask fit included shadows and incorrectly closed gaps.
  Nine continuous racks now use individually raised edges, the observed roof
  grid, underside rails, rear supports and a 12 × 8 cell pattern.
- **Digg:** moved the glass canopy's open leading arch behind the pointed roof
  tip using the master image and YU09/YU20. Rebuilt the lattice, thin glass
  section, interrupted window ribbons, rounded fascia and sign edges, and
  tubular reclining chairs. Calibrated roof, masonry and sign-blue responses.
- **Twitter/Blogger:** removed the duplicate elevated garden bridge and traced
  the single low crossing visible in the master image.
- **Intel:** remeasured three roof enclosures, the visible lower roof corner
  and edge, the window-course count and vertical phase, and roof-vector
  placement. Calibrated roof, glass, cream bands and enclosure blue.

The colour-probe reader now resolves materials on evaluated meshes, so bevel
and solidify modifiers cannot produce an out-of-range face index.
